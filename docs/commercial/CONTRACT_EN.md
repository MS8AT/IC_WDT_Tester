## 1. Scope and assignments

1.1. The Provider supplies consulting and information services, process analysis, configuration and software automation support under agreed Customer assignments. The Customer accepts and pays for the services.

1.2. Before each assignment, the parties sign an order using Appendix 2: actions, expected materials, dates, inputs, responsible persons, billable time cap and maximum price. A blank order creates no duty to perform or pay for undefined services.

1.3. New software development, hardware manufacture or supply, installation and work producing an independent tangible result require a separate specification and appropriate contract or addendum. Deliverables, acceptance, code rights and applicable work rules are defined separately. Hourly consulting does not transfer a complete system or exclusive rights.

1.4. Third-party licenses, cloud resources, equipment, travel and other expenses are not automatically included. Their scope, cap and payment arrangements require prior written agreement.

## 2. Service procedure

2.1. The Provider appoints a contact, records time and reports the date, task, duration and result of each billed action. Employees and subcontractors may be engaged; the Provider remains responsible for their actions and confidentiality.

2.2. The Customer supplies agreed data and materials lawfully available to it. System access is limited to the agreed task. Production infrastructure changes, device flashing and hardware actions require separately agreed authorisation.

2.3. Time is recorded in minutes of actual work. Idle waiting is not billable. Concurrent work by multiple specialists is billed separately for each only with prior written agreement.

2.4. Correction of confirmed Provider errors within the agreed assignment is not billable. Customer requirement changes are agreed as additional work.

2.5. At the time or price cap, further billable work pauses until a higher cap is agreed. Customer silence is not agreement.

## 3. Prices and discount

3.1. The base rate in this version is 272.51 BYN/hour. Legal entities registered in Belarus receive the contractual 88% discount under Appendix 1, giving 32.70 BYN/hour after rounding.

3.2. Assignment cost equals the agreed hourly rate multiplied by confirmed minutes divided by 60; round the final assignment amount mathematically to two decimal places. The order cap cannot be exceeded without written agreement.

3.3. Rates are final Customer prices. Applicable VAT is included, not added on top. The order, invoice and primary accounting document state VAT rate and amount or the basis for not charging it, according to the Provider actual tax regime. Accounting completes these fields before invoicing.

3.4. Prices and payments under this contract are in BYN. Reference conversion from USD creates no foreign-currency payment duty and does not change agreed rates when exchange rates change. New rates apply only to new orders after written agreement.

3.5. The discount covers hourly services under 1.1. Separate automation projects, hardware and third-party expenses are priced independently; the project starting price is not the price of every assignment under this contract.

## 4. Payment

4.1. Unless a signed order provides otherwise, the Customer pays an advance within the order cap within five banking days after receiving the invoice. Work starts after receipt of the advance and necessary inputs, within the agreed order schedule.

4.2. Pay by bank transfer in BYN to the Provider account in section 10. Purpose: payment for consulting and automation support under contract No. __ dated __, order __, invoice No. __ dated __; VAT details as stated in the invoice.

4.3. At completion, the advance is credited against accepted services. Unused funds are refunded within ten banking days after the final cost is determined; transfer to another order requires written agreement.

4.4. Invoices may be requested by Provider email or an agreed official Telegram contact. Messenger delivery does not replace scope agreement. QR or card payment is available only when the Provider supplies a valid instrument from its bank or payment provider. This contract does not confirm UnionPay or any specific service activation.

4.5. Account changes require notice signed by an authorised Provider representative. A message from an unknown account does not change contract payment details.

## 5. Reporting and acceptance

5.1. Within five working days after assignment completion, the Provider supplies the report, agreed materials and service acceptance certificate, containing mandatory accounting-law particulars.

5.2. Within five working days of receipt, the Customer signs acceptance or submits reasoned objections identifying unmet order terms. No response alone does not constitute signature.

5.3. The parties record valid objections and correction deadlines. Accepted parts may be documented separately. Unresolved disputes follow section 8.

5.4. Consulting acceptance confirms agreed actions and material delivery, not physical hardware tests, production readiness or economic outcomes unless separately agreed and verified.

## 6. Free software and IP rights

6.1. Use of the free software is not conditional on buying services. Charges cover agreed services and specialist time, not a donation or mandatory charge for the free software.

6.2. The software name, version and separate free license terms are supplied before use. This contract does not substitute for a missing license file or change rights-holder licenses.

6.3. After full payment, commissioned consulting reports and instructions owned by the Provider are licensed non-exclusively to the Customer for reproduction, storage, use and adaptation for its own business, and disclosure to its servicing contractor for those purposes subject to confidentiality. Territory: worldwide; term: the entire exclusive-right term. Remuneration is included in service prices. Exclusive rights are not assigned.

6.4. Terms for new code and source delivery are agreed separately before creation. Pre-existing components, third-party libraries, manufacturer documentation and PDFs retain rights-holder terms; the Provider supplies relevant notices and licenses.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1. Non-public technical, commercial and other information is used only to perform the contract and is not disclosed without a lawful basis or disclosing-party consent.

7.2. Confidential data may be sent to external cloud or model services only after separate agreement on service, data and processing terms. Personal-data processing on Customer instructions requires a prior appendix defining purposes, data, operations and safeguards; no such data is transferred beforehand.

7.3. Confidentiality lasts during the contract and three years afterward, or longer where legally protected for a longer period. It excludes lawfully public information.

## 8. Liability and disputes

8.1. Liability follows Belarusian law. The contract does not exempt the Provider from fault-based breach of agreed assignments or guarantee profit, payback or outcomes absent from the order.

8.2. A party facing an impediment promptly notifies the other and reasonably mitigates consequences. Legal grounds govern relief from liability; lack of funds or ordinary technical difficulty is not automatically such a ground.

8.3. Before court proceedings, a written claim must be submitted. The response period is fifteen working days after receipt unless the law requires another mandatory procedure. Disputes are heard by the competent Belarusian court under statutory jurisdiction rules.

## 9. Term, withdrawal and documents

9.1. The contract lasts one year from signature by both parties; settlements, completion of previously agreed assignments and surviving obligations continue until fulfilled.

9.2. The Customer may withdraw under Civil Code Article 736 by paying the Provider actual incurred expenses unless legislative acts provide otherwise. Services already performed enter settlement without double-counting costs. Unused advances are refunded less lawfully due sums; no penalty applies solely for withdrawal.

9.3. Provider unilateral withdrawal from service obligations is subject to full compensation of Customer losses under Article 736. Other termination or amendment grounds follow law and party agreement.

9.4. Contracts, orders and amendments are signed on paper or as electronic documents signed in the legally prescribed manner. Email and agreed Telegram contacts support operational communication; messages without established authority verification do not replace signatures or amend prices or payment details.

9.5. Appendix 1 Loyalty Programme Terms and signed Appendix 2 orders are integral parts. Customer details, number, actual date and first order must be completed before signing.
