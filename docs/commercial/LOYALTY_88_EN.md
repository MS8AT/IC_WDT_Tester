# Appendix 1: Loyalty programme terms for Belarusian companies

## Purpose

MS8AT GLOBAL offers a discount to lower Belarusian companies access costs for consulting and automation support. This is a Provider commercial programme, not a government benefit, budget subsidy or tax exemption.

## 2.1

Eligible Customers are legal entities registered in Belarus ordering services for their business. Eligibility is evidenced by Customer details and registration information in the signed contract.

## 2.2

The discount is 88% of the base hourly rate for consulting, information services, process analysis, configuration and software automation support under the contract.

## 2.3

Terms are the same for all Customers meeting 2.1. Discounts do not stack unless agreed in writing. A separate automation project purchase is not required for the discounted hourly rate.

## 2.4

Equipment, paid third-party licenses, cloud resources, travel and separately fixed-price projects are not automatically discounted. Their prices and any discounts are stated in the applicable order or separate contract.

## 3. Rate and calculation

Base: 272.51 BYN/hour. Discount: 88%; payable share: 12%. Rounded discounted rate: 32.70 BYN/hour. Calculation: 272.51 × 0.12 = 32.7012 BYN → 32.70 BYN. Rounded savings per hour: 239.81 BYN.

## Calculation continued

Orders use the rounded 32.70 BYN hourly rate and actual minutes under contract 3.2. Applicable VAT is included and itemised in documents. This appendix establishes no separate tax benefit.

## 4.1

For a Customer, this appendix takes effect upon contract signature and lasts for the contract term. Changes are not retroactive and do not increase an already agreed order price.

## 4.2

New programme terms apply to new contracts or by written party agreement. Exchange-rate changes alone do not alter the fixed BYN rate.

## 4.3

Before introduction, the Provider approves the actual base rate and discount by internal decision and checks applicable price regulation and economic justification. This draft does not certify an already approved price list or cost calculation.
